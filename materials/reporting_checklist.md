# Reporting checklist for an ETE or a TRC (from Table 6 of the tutorial)

| # | Item | What to report |
|---|------|----------------|
| 1 | Estimand | Which quantity (ETE or TRC), units, and comparator(s): active practice, no practice, both. |
| 2 | Access regime at test | What the participant had at the outcome measurement; no functionally equivalent aid. |
| 3 | Item novelty | Outcome items unseen in practice; how overlap was checked. |
| 4 | Transfer distance | Near / intermediate / far and how it was operationalised. |
| 5 | Delay | Time from end of practice to outcome, and its distribution. |
| 6 | Dose | Practice trials or sessions; within-session improvement in the comparator arm. |
| 7 | Removal probe | Availability randomised within participants? Item sets and orders balanced? Matched block in non-assisted arms? |
| 8 | Smallest effect of interest | Prespecified SESOI, equivalence bounds, which of the four decision outcomes was obtained. |
| 9 | Item sampling | Number of outcome items; whether items were treated as a random sample in the interval reported. |
| 10 | Preregistration and audit | Where protocol and analysis plan were registered; whether the implementation was audited before analysis. |
